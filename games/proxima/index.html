How it works

Somewhere on this chart is Proxima b, the one world in the cluster that can hold a colony. Your survey only counts if you bring it home.

  • Every arrival gives a carrier delay — how many jumps you are from Proxima b through the link network. It is exact, and it is the whole map.
  • Burning a link costs fuel proportional to its length. There is no refuelling except what you find.
  • A deep scan reveals what is sitting in every unvisited system linked to you. Cheaper than arriving somewhere with pirates in it.
  • Work the system once per system: depots give fuel, belts give ore, derelicts give whatever is left aboard.
  • Run out of fuel between stars, or lose the hull, and the expedition ends where it stands.
