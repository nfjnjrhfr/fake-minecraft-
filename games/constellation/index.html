Constellation

Shifting scoring
Claim a star. You, Vega and Rigel take turns — 8 stars each.
How it works

You are never told which pattern will pay. Three scoring variables are live at once, and one of them is swapped out after rounds 3 and 6 — a constellation built for the old rules may be worth nothing under the new ones.

  • Three claimants take turns taking one unclaimed star, 8 rounds.
  • Every live variable scores your whole constellation, every round. The board score you see is current, not final.
  • Stars have a colour (spectral class) and a magnitude of 1–4.
  • Adjacency means orthogonally touching on the grid; alignment means a straight run in any of the 8 directions.
