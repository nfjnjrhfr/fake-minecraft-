Art For All

14 lots · open outcry
You direct a museum with $1,200k and a board that expects a collection, not a pile.
How it works

Fourteen works come up, one at a time, in an open ascending auction. Raise or pass; when everyone else has passed, the work is yours at the price on the board.

  • Prestige is what critics score. Appeal is what gets people through the door.
  • Depth: three works of one movement pays +60, five pays +160.
  • Breadth: +12 for each distinct movement on the walls.
  • The museum with the highest total appeal takes the people's vote, +50.
  • Unspent money is not a virtue — it returns to the city at 1 point per $40k. But a bidding war you win at triple the estimate is worse.
  • Rival directors have tastes. Once you know what they collect, you know how far they will chase a lot.
