How it works

Nobody is playing against you. The fire follows the wind, the fuel and the slope, and it does the same thing whether you understand it or not.

  • Each hour every burning cell tries to light its neighbours. Chance goes up with fuel, up sharply downwind, and down with moisture.
  • A cell burns for two hours, then it is spent — and spent ground is the only reliable firebreak.
  • The wind shifts on its own. A break cut for yesterday's wind protects nothing.
  • You get 4 crew actions an hour. Water is immediate but temporary; cutting line is slow but permanent.
  • Score is the homes still standing first, unburned hectares second.
