Constellation 3D

4 × 4 × 4
Four stacked layers form one cube. Hover a star to see every line running through it.
How it works

The map from Constellation lifted into a cube. Depth is the whole difficulty: a star that looks isolated on its layer may be the middle of a diagonal running corner to corner through the stack.

  • You and Altair alternate, 16 stars each, out of 64.
  • There are 76 straight lines of four through the cube — 48 flat, 24 through the layers, 4 corner-to-corner diagonals.
  • A line pays only the claimant who holds it uncontested: 1 star = 1, two = 3, three = 7, all four = 15.
  • Contest a line and neither of you scores it, so blocking is worth real points.
